## Stenox is free

Starting with version 1.0.0, Stenox has no paid plans, license activation, free-tier restrictions, or recording time limits.

## Why the change?

Local speech and language models have improved substantially in both quality and efficiency. Stenox now uses those advances to provide a fast local-first experience without operating a paid managed model service.

<Note>
  **Developer's note:** Local speech and language models have advanced significantly in both quality and efficiency. Because of that progress, I have decided to move Stenox toward a local-first future and retire Stenox Cloud. From now on, Stenox will take advantage of on-device models such as Gemma for language processing and Parakeet for transcription, while continuing to adopt promising new models as they become available.
</Note>

## Local-first defaults

| Stage | Default | Where it runs |
|---|---|---|
| Transcription | Parakeet | On your Apple Silicon Mac |
| Language processing | Gemma on MLX | On your Apple Silicon Mac |

During onboarding, Stenox downloads and loads the required local models before your first recording. An internet connection is needed for that initial download; local use can then continue without sending your audio or text to Stenox.

## Stenox Cloud retirement

Stenox Cloud transcription, language processing, credits, and subscriptions are retired. They are no longer selectable in the app.

Existing receipts from purchases made before July 17, 2026 remain available through the legacy billing portal on the Stenox website. A previous purchase or license key is not required to use version 1.0.0 or later.

## Bring your own API keys

Supported third-party providers remain available. Add your own API key in Stenox settings when you want to use one.

<Warning>
  Third-party providers may apply their own prices, quotas, data policies, and terms. Stenox does not include or resell third-party usage.
</Warning>
