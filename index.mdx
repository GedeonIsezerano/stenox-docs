## Dictation that stays in your flow

Place your cursor in an editable field, use your [dictation shortcut](/setup/hotkeys), and speak. Stenox transcribes your recording, applies your enabled filler removal and word replacements, then inserts or copies the text. **Polish** is a separate action after dictation; an AI model is not required to start recording.

## Meetings with a source to return to

[Notetaker](/meetings/overview) captures microphone and system audio, organizes a conversation by speaker, and optionally drafts takeaways, decisions, tasks, and open questions. Edit the transcript, follow citations, add reference documents, or [ask a question](/meetings/references-and-ask) about the session.

## Choose where processing happens

Local speech and language models run on your Apple Silicon Mac after download. Cloud speech sends audio; cloud cleanup and subscription insights send the text and context needed for the chosen feature. Your choices for speech, dictation cleanup, and meeting insights are separate. Read [Privacy and data flow](/providers/local-privacy) to set them deliberately.
