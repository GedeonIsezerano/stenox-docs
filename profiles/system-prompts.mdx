---
title: "Writing instructions"
description: "Configure optional Polish instructions and distinguish them from meeting prompts."
---

Writing instructions guide **Polish**, the optional AI action after dictation. They do not change the audio recognizer or automatically rewrite every recording.

## Add a focused instruction

For a general cleanup model, open the profile editor or **Text cleanup** and set the supported writing-style or custom-instruction controls. Keep the instruction specific and check the result on representative dictation.

For example:

```text
Use short paragraphs. Preserve names, numbers, links, and the speaker's intent.
```

An instruction is a preference, not a guarantee of factual preservation. Review important text before sending it.

## Model limits

**S1-mini by Superwhisper** uses a fixed English cleanup pass and semi-formal style. Writing style, app overrides, and custom prompts do not apply. **Yooz-Light v3** also uses a specialized English correction path. Select a general model for broader writing instructions; see [Local AI cleanup](/providers/llm/mlx-local).

A prompt cannot add a language or capability that the selected model does not support. Word or phrase substitutions belong in [Vocabulary](/setup/vocabulary).

## Meeting prompts are separate

Use **Settings → Notetaker → Advanced** to set the focus, language, tone, and detail of future session insights. That prompt has a 4,000-character limit and retains source validation and protection for user edits. It is independent of the dictation profile's writing instructions.
