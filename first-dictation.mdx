## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- Installed Stenox and granted microphone and accessibility permissions
- An Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later
- An internet connection and free storage for the initial local model download

<Tip>
  Stenox 1.0 is free. You do not need an account, license key, paid plan, or Stenox Cloud credits.
</Tip>

## Prepare the local models

The default experience uses:

- **Parakeet** for local transcription
- **Gemma on MLX** for local language processing

Onboarding downloads and loads the required models before it enables the first recording. Keep Stenox open until setup reports that the models are ready.

<Note>
  The first launch takes longer because Stenox downloads model files and macOS compiles the local transcription model. Later launches reuse the local copies.
</Note>

## Record your first dictation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a text field">
    Open Notes, TextEdit, or another app and place the cursor where you want text to appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the menu bar">
    Confirm the Stenox icon is visible and that local model setup is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Press and hold your configured push-to-talk hotkey. The recording overlay and waveform should appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak naturally">
    Try: "Hello, this is my first local dictation with Stenox."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop and insert">
    Release the hotkey. Parakeet transcribes the audio, Gemma applies the selected processing, and Stenox inserts the result at your cursor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optional providers

Local models are the default, but you can connect a supported third-party transcription or language provider with your own API key.

<Warning>
  Third-party provider quotas, charges, and privacy policies apply to your account with that provider. Stenox does not resell or include that usage.
</Warning>

## Common first-time issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The first-recording button is disabled" icon="download">
    Local model setup is still running. Keep Stenox open, verify your internet connection and free storage, and wait until Parakeet is loaded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No text appears" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Confirm microphone and accessibility permissions, select a working input device, and make sure the cursor is in an editable field.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The hotkey does not work" icon="keyboard">
    Check for conflicts with other apps. Non-Apple keyboards may not expose the fn key, so configure a different shortcut if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A local language model does not initialize" icon="microchip">
    Quit and reopen the installed Stenox app. If the initial download was interrupted, open model settings and retry the download.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
