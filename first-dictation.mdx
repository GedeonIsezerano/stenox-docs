## Before you begin

Complete [installation](/installation), enable microphone and Accessibility permissions, and wait for the selected speech model to be ready. The new-install speech default is **Parakeet v2 (English)**. Profiles and saved settings can change it.

In V2, start the [seven-day trial or activate your license](/pricing/overview) before starting new work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place your cursor">
    Open Notes, TextEdit, your browser, or another app and click an editable field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Hold **Fn/Globe**, or press **Option–Space** to toggle recording. Use your saved shortcut if you changed it in **Settings → General**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Speak and stop">
    Say a short sentence. Release the hold shortcut, or press the toggle shortcut again. Stenox transcribes the audio and applies enabled filler removal and vocabulary replacements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the result">
    Text is inserted at the target or copied according to your output setting. The completion controls offer **Copy**, **Polish**, and **Undo** when available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Polish when you want to

Open **Text cleanup** and enable **Polish on demand** to choose a local or Gemini model. After dictation, click **Polish** to request a rewrite using that selection. With polishing disabled, the control leads you to its settings.

**Remove filler words** is independent of AI polishing. It removes clear hesitations without loading a language model. Dictation delivers this deterministic result first; it does not automatically run the selected AI model.

The default local cleanup selection is **Gemma 4 E4B**, with polishing initially off. [Local AI cleanup](/providers/llm/mlx-local) lists the available choices and their limits. Download and prepare a local model before using Polish; the Polish operation does not silently download a missing model or switch to a cloud provider.

## Review and recover

History retains the original dictation. When Stenox can verify the inserted text, **Undo** can remove a dictation or restore its pre-polish text. If the target or clipboard changed, Stenox may offer the polished text for **Copy** instead of replacing it. Review names, numbers, and important details before using an AI rewrite.

<Note>
  Dictation and meeting capture cannot run at the same time. End the active meeting before starting a dictation.
</Note>
