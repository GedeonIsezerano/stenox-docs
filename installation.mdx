## Check your Mac and build

The current app targets **Apple Silicon and macOS 15 or later**. Allow storage for each model you download, plus working memory for the models you run together. Download size is not a RAM requirement; Stenox does not publish a fixed per-model RAM guarantee.

Downloads are temporarily unavailable, and V2 remains unpublished. The steps below apply when a download is available; see [Release and access](/pricing/overview) for the current status. If you already use Stenox, read [Upgrading to V2](/guides/upgrading) before replacing it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download when available">
    Once downloads reopen, visit the [Stenox download page](https://stenox.app/download). Open the downloaded DMG and drag Stenox to Applications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the installed app">
    Open Stenox from Applications and follow its introduction and permission setup. After an upgrade, review your saved settings and permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up access in V2">
    In a compatible V2 app, choose **Start 7-day trial** or activate your existing license while online. See [trial and license access](/pricing/overview). Existing free V1.1 installations do not require this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow microphone and text insertion">
    Enable Stenox under **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** and **Accessibility**. Microphone access records your voice; Accessibility supports inserting text into other apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare speech recognition">
    New installations select **Parakeet v2 (English)**. Onboarding automatically starts preparing the selected local speech model, downloading missing files and loading cached ones. Keep Stenox open until it is ready; use **Try again** if preparation fails. Changing the local speech selection starts preparation for that model. Optional AI cleanup does not gate dictation readiness.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meeting permission

Notetaker also needs **Screen & System Audio Recording** access to capture the other side of a call. Current meeting setup captures microphone and system audio together. Grant the permission to the installed Stenox app and follow any macOS request to reopen it.

## First recording

Select your input in **Microphone**, open an editable text field, and try [your first dictation](/first-dictation). The default hold shortcut is **Fn/Globe**; **Option–Space** toggles recording. Saved shortcuts take precedence.

If setup fails, check model progress and free storage, then use the recovery steps in [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting).
