title: "Parakeet"

Parakeet runs locally through FluidAudio and Core ML on Apple Silicon. It needs a download before first use, then transcribes without sending audio to a speech service.

## Available models

| Stenox model | Model ID | Estimated download | Language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Parakeet v2 (English) | `parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2` | ~2.5 GB | English |
| Parakeet v3 (Multi) | `parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3` | ~2.5 GB | Multilingual |

**Parakeet v2 (English)** is the new-install speech default. Choose v3 for supported non-English speech. Do not assume that selecting a language makes the English-only v2 model multilingual.

## Set it up

1. Open **Profiles** and edit Default or a custom profile.
2. Choose **Parakeet (Local)** and the model you want.
3. Wait for download and loading to finish. Files already downloaded still need runtime preparation.
4. Record a short sample in the language you plan to use.

You can also pre-download models in **Settings → Models**. See [model management](/setup/models) for storage and loading behavior.

## Dictation and meetings

Dictation returns the transcript with enabled filler removal and user-defined replacements. AI rewriting is a separate **Polish** action. There is no automatic language-model cleanup pass merely because a cleanup model is selected.

Notetaker reuses the active Parakeet model and adds a local [speaker diarizer](/meetings/processing). It processes overlapping speech windows; words can arrive after a short delay. Overlapping speakers may be missed or assigned incorrectly, so review and correct important passages.

## Vocabulary

Use [Vocabulary](/setup/vocabulary) for explicit word or phrase replacements. The current Parakeet adapter does **not** send a boosted word list to its recognizer. Adding a replacement does not teach the model a name or guarantee that it recognizes previously unfamiliar speech.

## If recognition is poor

Check the selected model's language, input device, and microphone level. Try a short sample with less background noise. For meeting calls, use headphones to reduce duplicated system audio. [Whisper](/providers/transcription/whisperkit-local) and [Cohere Transcribe](/providers/transcription/cohere-local) are other local options; compare them on your own speech without assuming a universal accuracy ranking.
