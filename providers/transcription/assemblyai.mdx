title: "AssemblyAI"
description: "Configure AssemblyAI speech and understand streaming versus meeting requests."
---

Add an AssemblyAI key in **Settings → API Keys**, then select **AssemblyAI** in a speech profile. Choose its language and available region setting deliberately. Audio is sent to AssemblyAI.

## Models offered by this build

| Stenox label | ID |
| --- | --- |
| Universal-3.5 Pro Realtime | `universal-3-5-pro` |
| Universal Multilingual | `universal-streaming-multilingual` |
| Universal English | `universal-streaming-english` |

Streaming model selection applies to dictation. For files and Notetaker windows, the adapter uses **Universal-3.5 Pro**, with **Universal-2** as its configured fallback, and retains the chosen region and language. Do not infer a live streaming connection from a file or meeting request.

## Meeting behavior and privacy

Notetaker requests timed words and speaker labels. Labels are reconciled across requests and can be uncertain at boundaries. Correct the conversation when needed.

Stenox requests deletion of completed transcription jobs. Provider retention and account training settings still apply; this is not a zero-retention guarantee. Review [AssemblyAI's documentation](https://www.assemblyai.com/docs) and account controls.

## Account limits

Check current pricing, credits, quotas, and model access in the provider's console. Historical free-credit and per-minute amounts from older Stenox guides are not current promises. For failures, check the key, region, language, quota, and network connection before retrying.

## Diarized files

In **Transcribe file**, choose **Speakers & timestamps** to request a timed, speaker-labeled result from this provider. This mode preserves wording and disables cleanup/Polish; TXT, Markdown, JSON, SRT, and VTT exports are available. See [file transcription](/setup/file-transcription) for limits and recovery.
