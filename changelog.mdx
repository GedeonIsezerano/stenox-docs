## 2.1.0

### New features

- **Search your saved work.** Find dictations and meeting notes by words, phrases, or meaning. Filter by date, app, or content type and open the matching passage. Search runs locally, with an optional multilingual model.
- **Remember vocabulary corrections.** Save corrections to names and unfamiliar terms directly from supported text fields. Apply them to one app or across all apps, and manage them in Vocabulary.
- **Replay meeting recordings.** Keep audio locally for new Notetaker sessions, jump to transcript timestamps, adjust playback speed, and follow the conversation. Remove recordings without deleting transcripts or notes.

### Enhancements

- **Easier meeting-note editing.** Edit session notes directly, review updated AI drafts while preserving your edits, and export personal notes as Markdown.
- **Better Notetaker navigation.** Read earlier passages without being pulled back to the latest speech. Saved answers now show confirmation, and failed questions can be retried.
- **More compact dictation controls.** A smaller completion popup keeps Polish, Copy, and Undo within reach.
- **Clearer license settings.** See how many Macs are active on your license, with simpler activation and removal controls.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed file transcription being interrupted when switching pages, preserving selected files, settings, and results.
- Fixed question drafts being lost when navigating away from Ask.
- Fixed duplicate pasting in Chrome and insertion issues caused by focus changes.
- Fixed canceled insertion operations unexpectedly restoring focus.
- Corrected completion-popup dismissal timing and Polish controls that did not reflect current settings.
- Fixed History losing the distinction between original recognition and cleaned text in some dictation paths.
- Improved recovery of buffered meeting speech after capture interruptions and handling of unsaved work before quitting.
- Fixed permission checks disrupting recording shortcuts and cancellation.

## 2.0

- **Meetings with Notetaker.** Capture microphone and system audio, follow speaker-labeled conversations, and keep insights, personal notes, references, and answers together. Preparation questions guide insights, which you can also generate on demand. [Explore Notetaker](/meetings/overview).
- **Audio and video transcription.** Turn files into transcripts, with speaker labels and timed exports where the selected provider supports them. [Transcribe a file](/setup/file-transcription).
- **Dictation first, Polish when you choose.** Get your words into an app, then request an optional rewrite. Copy and Undo help you review the result; filler removal has its own setting. [Try dictation](/first-dictation).
- **Local and cloud choices.** Choose on-device speech and AI models, your own supported provider keys, or a connected subscription for meeting insights. Speech and insight processing have separate selections. [Choose providers](/providers/overview).
- **Updated setup and recovery.** Onboarding prepares your selected local speech model. Recover access to saved provider and licensing credentials from Settings. [Installation](/installation) · [Access recovery](/guides/upgrading#recover-provider-and-license-access).
- **Trial and license access.** Try the full app for seven days. Valid earlier paid licenses cover Stenox without another purchase and retain their original rights and device allowance. Existing free 1.x installations remain usable. [Release and access](/pricing/overview) · [Upgrade guide](/guides/upgrading).
