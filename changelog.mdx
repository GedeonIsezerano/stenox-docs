## 2.3.1

Faster, more natural local Polish, more flexible correction learning, and smoother dictation and search.

### Enhancements

- More efficient local search. Updated exact-text indexing and incremental index updates improve searching saved dictations and meeting notes.
- Faster local Polish. Gemma 4 E4B reduces repeated processing and keeps the model ready between requests.
- Improved local Polish wording, including context-supported corrections for misheard words and phrases.
- A more consistent Notetaker. Saved sessions blend with the app's window backdrop, with softer edges while scrolling.

### Bug fixes

- Remember now offers small manual corrections even when the replacement is an ordinary word.
- Reduced the delay before completion controls appear after dictation. Polish, Copy, and Undo become available once insertion is confirmed.
- Pressing Return or Enter in the original text field now dismisses the completion popup without interrupting typing.
- Fixed Ask Stenox rejecting otherwise valid answers when a model wraps its response in a JSON code block.

## 2.3.0

Meet the Stenox CLI, a refreshed native interface, and faster local Polish.

### New features

- Stenox CLI. Manage dictation profiles, check setup, and archive or restore profiles from Terminal or an agent. It comes with Stenox and stays up to date with the app.

### Enhancements

- A refreshed native interface. Updated artwork and native glass surfaces give Stenox a clearer, more consistent look.
- Faster local Polish with S1-mini and Yooz.
- Use your existing AI tools. Choose a compatible Codex app or CLI, or Claude Code, for Polish and meeting insights with an eligible subscription.

## 2.2.1

### Enhancements

- **Easier profile editing.** Collapsible sections keep transcription, enhancement, and auto-activation settings organized, with more compact Polish controls.
- **Recognizable app profiles.** App-specific profiles and activation rules now display application names and icons.
- **Safer text insertion.** Improved handling of focus changes when inserting dictation or replacing text with Polish.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed Polish copying its result instead of updating the dictated passage in some supported text fields. Undo can now restore the original dictation in those fields.
- Fixed previous clipboard contents not being restored after some successful Polish replacements.

## 2.2.0

### New features

- **Online research with CLI tools.** When you use Codex, Claude Code, or Antigravity for meeting AI, Ask Stenox and generated insights can search online, with source links and clearly labeled web context. Online search is available only through these CLI tools; local models do not browse the web.
- **Polish with your subscription.** Choose Codex, Claude Code, or Antigravity for optional dictation polishing, with separate model and reasoning preferences from meeting insights.

### Enhancements

- **Exclude unwanted speech from insight generation.** Exclude a speaker or an individual passage from insights, answers, and AI notes while keeping the transcript and recording. Restore exclusions at any time.
- **Faster local search.** Improved indexing and retrieval reduce delays and timeouts when searching larger collections of dictations and meeting notes.
- **More flexible title matching.** Find saved meetings with variations in spacing or punctuation, with typo recovery for otherwise empty keyword searches.
- **Clearer app layouts.** Refined Settings, Vocabulary, and activity views, with more compact saved-meeting rows and search controls.
- **Freshened up the logo.**

### Bug fixes

- Fixed long meeting titles and toolbar controls being clipped when Ask Stenox is open.
- Fixed Polish model selections being replaced when switching profiles or accounts, or switching between local and subscription providers.

## 2.1.0

### New features

- **Search your saved work.** Find dictations and meeting notes by words, phrases, or meaning. Filter by date, app, or content type and open the matching passage. Search runs locally, with an optional multilingual model.
- **Remember vocabulary corrections.** Save corrections to names and unfamiliar terms directly from supported text fields. Apply them to one app or across all apps, and manage them in Vocabulary.
- **Replay meeting recordings.** Keep audio locally for new Notetaker sessions, jump to transcript timestamps, adjust playback speed, and follow the conversation. Remove recordings without deleting transcripts or notes.

### Enhancements

- **Easier meeting-note editing.** Edit session notes directly, review updated AI drafts while preserving your edits, and export personal notes as Markdown.
- **Better Notetaker navigation.** Read earlier passages without being pulled back to the latest speech. Saved answers now show confirmation, and failed questions can be retried.
- **More compact dictation controls.** A smaller completion popup keeps Polish, Copy, and Undo within reach.
- **Clearer license settings.** See how many Macs are active on your license, with simpler activation and removal controls.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed file transcription being interrupted when switching pages, preserving selected files, settings, and results.
- Fixed question drafts being lost when navigating away from Ask.
- Fixed duplicate pasting in Chrome and insertion issues caused by focus changes.
- Fixed canceled insertion operations unexpectedly restoring focus.
- Corrected completion-popup dismissal timing and Polish controls that did not reflect current settings.
- Fixed History losing the distinction between original recognition and cleaned text in some dictation paths.
- Improved recovery of buffered meeting speech after capture interruptions and handling of unsaved work before quitting.
- Fixed permission checks disrupting recording shortcuts and cancellation.

## 2.0

- **Meetings with Notetaker.** Capture microphone and system audio, follow speaker-labeled conversations, and keep insights, personal notes, references, and answers together. Preparation questions guide insights, which you can also generate on demand. [Explore Notetaker](/meetings/overview).
- **Audio and video transcription.** Turn files into transcripts, with speaker labels and timed exports where the selected provider supports them. [Transcribe a file](/setup/file-transcription).
- **Dictation first, Polish when you choose.** Get your words into an app, then request an optional rewrite. Copy and Undo help you review the result; filler removal has its own setting. [Try dictation](/first-dictation).
- **Local and cloud choices.** Choose on-device speech and AI models, your own supported provider keys, or a connected subscription for meeting insights. Speech and insight processing have separate selections. [Choose providers](/providers/overview).
- **Updated setup and recovery.** Onboarding prepares your selected local speech model. Recover access to saved provider and licensing credentials from Settings. [Installation](/installation) · [Access recovery](/guides/upgrading#recover-provider-and-license-access).
- **Trial and license access.** Try the full app for seven days. Valid earlier paid licenses cover Stenox without another purchase and retain their original rights and device allowance. Existing free 1.x installations remain usable. [Release and access](/pricing/overview) · [Upgrade guide](/guides/upgrading).
