Start with [Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting) for permission, licensing, model, dictation, or meeting issues. Read [Upgrading to V2](/guides/upgrading) for upgrade preparation and credential recovery. Check [Release and access](/pricing/overview) if your build does not have a feature shown in these docs.

Email [support@stenox.app](mailto:support@stenox.app) or visit the [support page](https://stenox.app/support). Include the app and macOS versions, the affected feature, selected provider/model, and what happened.

For third-party limits or billing, also check that provider's console. Stenox app access and external provider usage are separate.

[Send useful feedback](/support/feedback) explains what to include and what to leave out.
