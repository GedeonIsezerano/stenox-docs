---
title: "Set up local processing"
description: "Prepare local speech, optional cleanup, and meeting insights for use on your Mac."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose local speech">
    In your active profile, select Parakeet, Whisper, or Cohere Transcribe and a supported language. Wait for the model to download and load.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose optional cleanup">
    Leave Polish off if you only need transcription, filler removal, and replacements. If you want Polish, select **Local** in Text cleanup and prepare its model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up Notetaker">
    Choose **On-device** insights in Settings → Notetaker or the session's **Insights model** menu. Allow the separate insight and speaker models to prepare before relying on them. Turning automatic insights off is separate: **Generate insights** still uses the selected provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the active configuration">
    Profiles can change speech and cleanup choices. Test a short dictation or meeting and inspect its status. Confirm all needed downloads are complete before going offline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Local inference does not mean the app never connects to the network. Downloads, calendar access, optional services, and V2 trial and paid-license verification are separate. Read [Privacy and data flow](/providers/local-privacy) and [Release and access](/pricing/overview).

Personal notes and preparation questions guide insights and refinement and are included in those requests. Choose **On-device** to keep that processing local, including for Google-linked sessions. Choose local speech as well to process recorded audio on your Mac. Switching back from cloud processing or removing notes does not recall earlier transfers or sign out the provider.
