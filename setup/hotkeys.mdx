---
title: "Keyboard shortcuts"
description: "Configure dictation and meeting shortcuts and understand their actions."
---

Open **Settings → General** to review or change your shortcuts. Saved choices take precedence over these new-install defaults.

| Action | Default | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hold to dictate | Fn / Globe | Hold while speaking, release to finish. |
| Toggle dictation | Option–Space | Press to start, press again to finish. |
| Cancel recording | Escape | Cancel the current dictation. |
| Undo dictation | Option–Z | Undo when Stenox has a valid insertion or clipboard context. |
| Start / end meeting | Control–Option–M | Start a Notetaker session or end the active one. |

The meeting shortcut can start directly without the setup sheet. A meeting and dictation cannot record simultaneously.

## If a shortcut does not work

Check the saved chord, conflicts with macOS or another app, and whether Stenox is running. Some external keyboards do not expose Fn/Globe as expected; choose another available shortcut. Confirm that speech preparation and required permissions are complete.

Undo depends on the target and whether Stenox can verify the text it inserted. It is not a general undo command for arbitrary changes in another app. Use the target app's own undo when appropriate.

The current build does not document the old right-Command/right-Option feedback shortcut as an available workflow. [Send feedback](/support/feedback) through the supported contact options.
